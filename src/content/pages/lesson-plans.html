Stories That Keep Teaching

Turn Story Time Into Discovery

Bonni's books invite children to wonder, talk, create, and connect. These flexible lesson plans help teachers, librarians, homeschool families, and caregivers carry those conversations beyond the final page with age-appropriate activities built around literacy, imagination, and real-world discovery.

Choose a Learning Level

A Story for Every Stage

Each plan can stand alone or grow into a longer unit, making it easy to fit Bonni's stories into a classroom, library program, or learning day at home.

  • Pre-K–K

    Little Listeners

    Build listening skills and story confidence through read-aloud prompts, picture walks, movement, and simple hands-on play.

    • Characters and feelings
    • Sequencing through pictures
    • Drawing and dramatic play
  • Grades 1–3

    Curious Readers

    Strengthen comprehension while children connect a story's choices, setting, and themes to their own experiences.

    • Prediction and recall
    • Vocabulary in context
    • Nature and family connections
  • Grades 4–6

    Growing Storytellers

    Invite older readers to look closely at craft, point of view, and theme before shaping stories and projects of their own.

    • Theme and character analysis
    • Creative and reflective writing
    • Research and discussion
