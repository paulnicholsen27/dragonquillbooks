Dragon Quill Books (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when you visit our website, sign up for our newsletter, or contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us.
  • Information you send through email inquiries or other direct communication.
  • Website usage information, such as your browser type, pages visited, and general location data, to help us improve the experience of our site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions and comments.
  • Send newsletters, updates, and marketing communications only when you have opted in.
  • Improve the content, design, and functionality of our website.
  • Monitor and protect the security and integrity of our site.

Newsletter and Email Communications

When you subscribe through our website, your information may be processed by a third-party email service provider, such as Mailchimp, to deliver newsletters and related updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in each email or by contacting us directly at {{ client.email }}.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site, remember preferences, and improve performance. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, although some parts of the website may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to support website operations, email delivery, analytics, and marketing. These providers are required to handle your information securely and only as necessary to provide their services. We do not sell your personal information.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is designed for families and young readers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental consent or as permitted by applicable law. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has submitted personal information without consent, please contact us and we will take prompt steps to remove it.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, though doing so may limit your ability to subscribe to newsletters or contact us. If you would like to update, access, or delete any personal information we have collected, please contact us at {{ client.email }}.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at {{ client.email }}.
