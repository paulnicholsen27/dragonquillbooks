Meet the Author

Bring the Joy of Storytelling to Your Students

A visit from Bonni Lyn Kuhn turns reading into a shared adventure. Through an animated read-aloud, a peek into the writing process, and plenty of student participation, young readers discover how ideas become books and why their own stories matter.

More Than a Read-Aloud

Each visit is shaped for the age and interests of your group, with a warm, interactive format that welcomes curious questions and creative thinking.

  • 01

    Story Time

    Students experience Bonni's books read by the author, with moments to predict, notice, laugh, and wonder together.

  • 02

    Behind the Book

    Bonni shares where story ideas begin and how imagination, drafting, and persistence help turn an idea into a finished book.

  • 03

    Questions and Creativity

    A lively question-and-answer session gives students space to explore authorship and begin imagining stories of their own.
